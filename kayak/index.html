          Before you launch

          • This is a planning aid built on computer forecast models. It cannot see local effects like tide races, overfalls, headland acceleration or surf dumping on a steep beach.
          • Check the official marine forecast and tide times for your area (in the UK: Met Office inshore waters forecast and Admiralty EasyTide).
          • Dress for immersion in the water temperature, not the air temperature.
          • Tell someone your plan and expected return time. Carry a way to call for help (VHF radio or phone in a waterproof case).
