Military Features
STAR NG has been designed to fit specific military Air Traffic
Control concerns:
3D for non cooperative targets
Detection and tracking of hovering helicopters
Highly maneuverable targets
Frequency agility and Least Jammed Frequency
Jamming strobe detection
CyberSecurity Onboard
Based on international standards the STAR NG / RSM NG is
CyberSecure by design and provides a unique interface for
the control of CyberSecurity functions, whilst always preserving
radar operational behavior thanks to a CyberSecurity Virtual
Machine.
Military & Civil Cooperation
STAR NG co-mounted with RSM NG is the only radar able to
combine the needs for ATC of both environments so that it can
provide the performances of a military system with the reliability
and cost of a civilian system.
Ready for T
oday and Future
Environmental Constraints
STAR NG can be operated under adverse conditions thanks to
frequency agility, 4G/5G filter or interference map.
STAR NG has a dedicated, and field proven, processing to
mitigate windfarm impact.
Ease of Daily Operation
STAR NG /RSM NG has been reengineered in order to limit life-
cycle costs thanks to a very compact design, fail soft capability,
hot switch replacement and an increased reliability, reducing
preventive maintenance to less than a 8 hours per year.
